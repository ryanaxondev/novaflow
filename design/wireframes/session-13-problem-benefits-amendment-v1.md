# Problem Decision

The approved structure is:

```text
The three approved pain points remain present and equally represented.

The current three isolated floating cards may be replaced by
one open fragmentation composition.
```

The fragmentation composition may use:

- three distinct content regions,
- separated operational nodes,
- interrupted or incomplete connection paths,
- structural grid alignment,
- varied spatial positioning,
- one shared visual field.

Each pain-point region must remain open: partial rules and anchors may establish
its position, but no region may use a complete enclosing card border or become
one of three floating white boxes.

It must preserve:

- three readable pain-point headings,
- three complete descriptions,
- equal semantic importance,
- clear reading order,
- independent accessibility,
- responsive linearization.

It must not:

- merge the three pain points into one paragraph,
- hide any pain point,
- imply a fourth problem,
- resemble a security breach,
- resemble a system error screen,
- use animation as the only evidence of fragmentation.

---

# Benefits Decision

The approved structure is:

```text
The four approved benefits remain present and equal in Product meaning.

The current four equal floating cards may be replaced by
a connected modular outcome register or outcome band.
```

The Benefits composition may use:

- connected rows,
- modular panels,
- varied visual density,
- shared system alignment,
- controlled emphasis for reading rhythm.

It must preserve:

- all four benefit headings,
- all four descriptions,
- equal Product importance,
- understandable reading order,
- mobile reflow,
- clear text ownership.

Controlled visual variation must not imply that one benefit is a new primary
Product promise unless separately approved.

---

# Mobile Contract

At narrow widths:

- Problem must linearize into a clear sequence,
- Benefits must avoid four large repeated cards,
- no horizontal scrolling is required,
- all text remains visible,
- connectors may simplify or disappear,
- meaning must remain without decorative lines,
- content order remains the approved order.
