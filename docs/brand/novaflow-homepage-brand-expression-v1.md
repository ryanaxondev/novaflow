# Approved Mark Usage

The canonical standalone mark may be used:

- beside the unchanged `NovaFlow` text wordmark in Navbar,
- beside the unchanged `NovaFlow` text wordmark in Footer,
- inside Product-preview chrome when the mark represents NovaFlow itself.

## Navbar Usage

The approved Navbar brand presentation is:

```text
[approved geometric N mark] [unchanged NovaFlow text wordmark]
```

Requirements:

- the text `NovaFlow` remains visible and unchanged,
- the mark remains a separate SVG asset,
- mark and text align as a lockup through layout only,
- no combined replacement asset is created,
- clear space and responsive size follow the approved high-fidelity references,
- the brand link destination remains `/`.

## Footer Usage

The approved Footer brand presentation may repeat:

```text
[approved geometric N mark] [unchanged NovaFlow text wordmark]
```

Requirements:

- the approved brand statement remains unchanged,
- copyright remains unchanged,
- the mark does not dominate the Footer,
- Final CTA and Footer must form a coherent visual closure.

## Product-Preview Usage

The mark may appear inside Product-preview chrome only when it identifies:

- NovaFlow as the illustrated Product,
- a NovaFlow system shell,
- a compact Product navigation surface.

It must not appear:

- on every Product card,
- inside unrelated third-party integration nodes,
- as a workflow step,
- as an approval state,
- as a decorative pattern.

- create a new combined logo asset.


# Approved Background Use

## Light Surfaces

On Base, Subtle, or Operational light surfaces:

- use the canonical `#2563EB` mark,
- preserve its transparent background,
- maintain enough clear space from text and controls,
- avoid surrounding blue decoration that reduces recognition.

## Deep Navy Surfaces

On Brand navy `#0B1220`:

- the mark remains `#2563EB`,
- the mark must not be recolored white or cyan,
- the rendered graphical contrast must be validated at final size,
- high-fidelity references must confirm that the mark remains recognizable.

The contrast between `#2563EB` and `#0B1220` is approximately 3.62:1, but rendered non-text contrast must still be validated in context.

## Prohibited Backgrounds

Do not place the mark over:

- photographs,
- gradients,
- busy Product content,
- animated backgrounds,
- Signal cyan fields,
- surfaces that reduce edge clarity.

# Approved Homepage Brand Devices

```text
Nova blue signal
deep navy Product workbench
fine operational grid
connected workflow line
structured state nodes
modular system surfaces
```

These devices form the approved homepage brand language.

They are not independent decoration.

---

# Nova Blue Signal

## Meaning

Nova blue communicates:

- active operation,
- primary action,
- selected emphasis,
- approved mark identity,
- meaningful workflow progression.

## Allowed Use

- primary CTA,
- active workflow step,
- recommended Pro emphasis,
- selected Product path,
- focus treatment after accessibility validation.

## Prohibited Use

- every icon,
- every heading,
- large body backgrounds without Product meaning,
- decorative saturation,
- replacement for all neutral hierarchy.

---

# Deep Navy Product Workbench

## Meaning

Deep navy creates a focused environment for Product evidence.

It represents:

- operational concentration,
- system depth,
- resolved Product structure.

## Allowed Use

- Hero Product workbench,
- terminal Final CTA,
- restrained Footer continuation,
- limited Product chrome.

## Prohibited Use

- full dark-mode homepage,
- every Product panel,
- repeated dark bands,
- gaming or cyberpunk atmosphere.

---

# Fine Operational Grid

## Meaning

The grid communicates:

- alignment,
- repeatability,
- shared system structure,
- a reliable spatial framework.

## Allowed Use

- Product canvases,
- workflow transformations,
- restrained terminal motif,
- alignment between narrative and Product evidence.

## Prohibited Use

- arbitrary decoration,
- circuit-board imitation,
- exact imitation of another company's grid,
- high-density background behind long-form text.

Decorative grid lines must be hidden from assistive technology.

---

# Connected Workflow Line

## Meaning

The workflow line communicates:

- sequence,
- handoff,
- dependency,
- progression,
- continuity of one recurring process.

## Allowed Use

- Problem-to-Solution transformation,
- Product workflow views,
- cross-functional scenario,
- resolved-flow motif in Final CTA.

## Prohibited Use

- decorative line flourishes,
- connections without endpoints,
- lines that imply unapproved Product functionality,
- motion-only meaning.

---

# Structured State Nodes

## Meaning

Nodes represent:

- workflow steps,
- owners,
- approvals,
- guidance,
- system states.

## Rules

- each node must have a Product or narrative meaning,
- state must not rely on color alone,
- node shape and labels must remain readable at mobile sizes,
- nodes must not become generic icon tiles repeated across the page.

---

# Modular System Surfaces

## Meaning

Modular surfaces communicate that a repeatable system is made of connected responsibilities rather than disconnected cards.

## Rules

- boundaries should correspond to a discrete Product object,
- modules may align or overlap when that relationship is meaningful,
- surfaces must share a restrained depth hierarchy,
- modules must not become decorative Bento-grid filler.

---

# Icon Policy

Lucide remains allowed for semantic interface actions and supporting Product concepts.

Appropriate use includes:

- menu and close controls,
- disclosure chevrons,
- workflow actions,
- semantic state support,
- compact Product utilities.

Lucide icons must not carry the entire NovaFlow identity.

Section identity should come from:

- composition,
- Product visualization,
- surface hierarchy,
- workflow relationships,
- brand color,
- the approved mark where authorized.

Do not:

- create a unique icon tile for every paragraph by default,
- substitute icons for Product evidence,
- use icons without text when their meaning is not universally clear,
- use decorative icons as the only expression of a section.

# Approved CTA Brand Hierarchy

```text
Navbar CTA:
compact Nova-blue primary

Hero CTA:
dominant Nova-blue primary

Pricing Pro CTA:
Nova-blue primary

Starter and Enterprise:
secondary treatment

Final CTA:
highest-contrast context with one primary action

Footer:
text link only
```

This specification changes no CTA label or destination.

## CTA Requirements

- Primary CTA text must meet contrast requirements.
- Nova blue must remain a controlled signal.
- Final CTA receives the strongest contextual contrast, not an additional action.
- Starter and Enterprise must remain visually available and usable.
- CTA hierarchy must remain understandable without color alone.

---

# Responsive Brand Rules

At small widths:

- preserve the unchanged text wordmark,
- retain the mark only if the approved lockup remains readable,
- do not remove the text wordmark to save space,
- do not crop or compress the mark,
- do not reduce touch-target size,
- preserve MobileNavbar behavior,
- simplify grid and connector density before reducing brand clarity.

Responsive implementation must preserve the approved lockup without harming
navigation usability, mark clarity, or the unchanged text wordmark.

---

# Accessibility Guardrails

- Preserve readable text contrast beside the mark.
- Validate mark recognition on light and navy surfaces.
- Do not rely on the mark as the only accessible name for the brand link.
- Preserve visible `NovaFlow` text.
- Hide decorative grid and connectors from assistive technology.
- Do not use color alone to identify active workflow states.
- Verify focus styles against every approved surface.
- Avoid repeated mark usage that creates assistive or visual noise.

---

# Performance Guardrails

- Reuse the existing canonical SVG.
- Do not create duplicate raster logo assets.
- Do not inline multiple independent copies of complex SVG markup if normal asset reuse is sufficient.
- Do not add an icon or animation dependency.
- Do not animate the mark.
- Preserve explicit sizing to avoid layout shift.
