# Purpose

This document freezes the messaging and narrative connection between the homepage Benefits and Features sections.

It defines what the sections communicate—not their layout, visual execution, or engineering implementation.

It introduces no new product decisions.

---

# Narrative Sequence

```text
Solution
How does NovaFlow make work repeatable?

↓

Benefits
What business outcomes can this approach support?

↓

Features
Which product capabilities support those outcomes?

↓

Social Proof
Why should visitors trust that the product can deliver?
```

Benefits and Features form one narrative unit but remain separate implementation features.

Benefits explains the value of the NovaFlow approach before Features explains the product mechanisms behind it.

---

# Narrative Foundation

- NovaFlow helps growing teams build repeatable operational systems.
- Repeatable systems should improve consistency, onboarding, collaboration, and operational scalability.
- Benefits communicates outcomes experienced by the team or organization.
- Features communicates the concrete product capabilities that support those outcomes.
- Product capabilities are evidence for previously introduced outcomes; they must not invent new business promises.
- NovaFlow differentiates itself by improving how recurring work is performed—not by adding another layer of task management.
- Clarity, repeatability, and simplicity take precedence over feature density.
- Repeatable systems can support more consistent execution, faster onboarding, clearer collaboration, and scalable operations.

---

# Approved Messaging Decisions

Product review approves the following v1 messaging decisions:

- Use **Consistent Execution** instead of **Consistency** to express the Benefit as an observable organizational outcome.
- Use **Scalable Operations** instead of **Sustainable Growth** to keep the outcome within NovaFlow's operational scope and avoid implying broader business-growth results.
- Use **Workflow Design** instead of **Process Management** to emphasize the creation of clear, repeatable workflows rather than broad process administration.
- Use **Guided Execution** instead of **Task & Project Management** to preserve NovaFlow's distinction from general-purpose task-management tools.
- Use **Centralized Knowledge** instead of **Knowledge Centralization** for clearer, more natural customer-facing language.
- Use **Automation & Approvals** instead of **Automation & Workflows** to distinguish execution automation from workflow design and describe a concrete operational mechanism.

These decisions refine the wording and positioning of existing approved capability categories. They do not expand product scope.

# Features Section

## Narrative Role

Present the concrete NovaFlow capabilities that support the previously introduced business outcomes.

Visitors should understand what the product provides without interpreting NovaFlow as another general-purpose task-management tool.

## User Question

> What does NovaFlow actually do?

## Core Message

NovaFlow brings structured workflows, guided execution, shared operational knowledge, automation, reporting, and integrations together to help teams run recurring work consistently.

## Visible Copy in v1

The approved v1 Features section should display:

- one section headline,
- six product capabilities,
- one concise description for each capability.

The product preview supports this copy visually but does not replace it.

## Headline

Capabilities built for repeatable operations

## Supporting Copy

No separate supporting paragraph is required in the approved v1 layout.

The headline and capability descriptions should explain the product clearly without relying on the product preview.

## Product Capabilities

### Workflow Design

Turn recurring processes into clear, structured workflows teams can follow.

### Guided Execution

Keep steps, ownership, and handoffs clear from start to finish.

### Centralized Knowledge

Keep instructions, context, and operational guidance connected to the work.

### Automation & Approvals

Automate repeatable steps, handoffs, and approvals where consistency matters.

### Analytics & Reporting

Give teams shared visibility into workflow progress and operational performance.

### Integrations

Connect NovaFlow with the tools teams already use.

## Audience Takeaway

> NovaFlow combines the practical capabilities required to define, run, improve, and scale repeatable operational workflows.

# Outcome-to-Capability Mapping

This mapping defines the primary narrative relationship between approved business outcomes and product capabilities.

It identifies primary support rather than every possible product relationship.

This is internal messaging guidance and is not visible copy in v1.

| Business Outcome     | Supported By                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Consistent Execution | Workflow Design, Guided Execution, Centralized Knowledge, Automation & Approvals, Analytics & Reporting |
| Faster Onboarding    | Workflow Design, Guided Execution, Centralized Knowledge                                                |
| Better Collaboration | Guided Execution, Centralized Knowledge, Analytics & Reporting, Integrations                            |
| Scalable Operations  | Workflow Design, Automation & Approvals, Analytics & Reporting, Integrations                            |

The mapping follows these rules:

- Every Benefit must describe an organizational outcome rather than a product mechanism.
- Every Feature must support at least one approved Benefit.
- A Feature may support multiple Benefits.
- Features must not introduce additional business outcomes that the Benefits section has not established.
- The visible Benefits and Features copy should remain understandable without displaying this mapping directly.
- The mapping does not imply guaranteed numerical results or unsupported performance claims.

# Messaging Boundaries

The messaging must:

- continue the Solution narrative without repeating Standardize, Systematize, and Scale word for word,
- express Benefits in the language of organizational outcomes,
- express Features in the language of concrete product capabilities,
- connect every capability to one or more approved outcomes,
- preserve NovaFlow's focus on repeatable operational systems,
- remain calm, credible, and specific,
- keep visible copy concise enough to support fast scanning,
- prepare visitors for Social Proof without making validation claims prematurely.

The messaging must not:

- describe a capability as though it were a business outcome,
- turn Benefits into a feature list,
- turn Features into a second Benefits section,
- position NovaFlow as a general-purpose task or project-management product,
- introduce unsupported efficiency, revenue, productivity, or growth claims,
- imply guaranteed results,
- add capabilities solely to fill the visual layout,
- repeat the Problem or Solution copy unnecessarily,
- introduce customer evidence, metrics, pricing, or CTAs before their approved sections.

---

# Success Criteria

The messaging is successful when a first-time visitor can understand:

- which practical business outcomes NovaFlow's approach is intended to support,
- the difference between an outcome and a product capability,
- what NovaFlow concretely provides,
- how the capabilities connect to the previously introduced outcomes,
- why Features logically follow Benefits,
- why Social Proof logically follows the product claims.

The artifact must also satisfy these conditions:

- Every visible Benefit is written as an outcome.
- Every visible Feature is written as a capability.
- Every capability maps to at least one approved outcome.
- No capability creates a new product position or business promise.
- Benefits and Features remain distinct without feeling disconnected.
- All visible copy remains understandable without relying on icons, screenshots, or diagrams.
